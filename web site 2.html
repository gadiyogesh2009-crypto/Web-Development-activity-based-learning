Healthy Recipes

🥗

Vegetable Salad

Approx. 180 calories

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot, lemon and lettuce.

Preparation: Chop vegetables, mix together and add lemon juice.

🥤

Fruit Smoothie

Approx. 220 calories

Ingredients: Banana, milk, strawberries and oats.

Preparation: Blend all ingredients until smooth.

🥣

Healthy Oatmeal

Approx. 250 calories

Ingredients: Oats, milk, nuts and banana.

Preparation: Cook oats with milk and add fruit and nuts.

🫘

Sprouts Salad

Approx. 160 calories

Ingredients: Sprouts, onion, tomato and lemon.

Preparation: Mix the ingredients and serve fresh.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Choose whole grains and fiber-rich foods.
  • Limit excess sugar, salt and highly processed foods.
  • Eat balanced meals and maintain regular meal times.
